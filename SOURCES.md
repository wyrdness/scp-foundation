## Verification Notes
The SCP Foundation is a documented fictional creation with clear origins in 2007 on 4chan. While the stories are fictional, the project itself is a real cultural phenomenon representing modern collaborative storytelling. All content is explicitly marked as creative fiction on the official wiki.

---

**Research Guidelines**: The SCP Foundation is fiction presented as fiction. Academic interest lies in its collaborative nature, cultural impact, and role as modern folklore. Approach as a cultural phenomenon and creative writing project, not as claims of real anomalies.
