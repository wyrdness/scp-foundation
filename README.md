# SCP Foundation

## Overview
The **SCP Foundation** is a collaborative fiction project featuring a fictional organization that secures, contains, and protects anomalous objects and entities. Created in 2007 on 4chan's /x/ board, it has become one of the internet's most influential horror fiction projects.

**Status**: Documented fictional creation  
**Category**: Modern Internet Folklore  
**Period**: 2007-Present  
**Origin**: 4chan /x/ Board

## Description

### The Fiction
The SCP Foundation presents itself as a secret international organization dedicated to containing paranormal phenomena. In the fictional universe, the Foundation operates hidden facilities worldwide to secure anomalous objects (SCPs), contain dangerous entities, and protect humanity from supernatural threats they cannot understand.

### Real Origins
The project began in 2007 when an anonymous user posted "SCP-173" to 4chan's /x/ paranormal board. This original creepypasta described a concrete statue that could only move when not being observed. The concept resonated with users, who began creating their own numbered SCP entries, eventually migrating to a dedicated wiki format for easier organization and collaboration.

### Format and Style
- **Clinical Documentation**: Written as mock-scientific containment procedures
- **Numbered System**: Anomalies designated as SCP-XXX (e.g., SCP-173, SCP-682)
- **Classification**: Objects rated by threat level (Safe, Euclid, Keter, etc.)
- **Collaborative**: Thousands of writers contributing interconnected stories
- **Creative Commons**: Content shared under CC licensing

## Cultural Impact

### Internet Folklore Phenomenon
Though documented as fiction, the SCP Foundation has spread through internet culture in ways similar to traditional folklore:
- **Viral Spread**: Stories shared across platforms
- **Community Belief**: Some readers initially unaware of fictional nature
- **Adaptation**: Constant reinterpretation and expansion
- **Multimedia**: Games, videos, artwork, and merchandise

### Influence
- Inspired video games (SCP: Containment Breach, SCP: Secret Laboratory)
- Generated thousands of fan works and derivative content
- Influenced horror writing style on the internet
- Created a model for collaborative worldbuilding

## Key Characteristics
- **Mock-Scientific Tone**: Clinical, detached documentation style
- **Redaction**: Heavy use of [REDACTED] and [DATA EXPUNGED]
- **Containment Procedures**: Detailed security protocols
- **Object Classes**: Classification system for anomalies
- **Cross-References**: Interconnected universe of entries
- **Community Guidelines**: Strict quality control and voting system

## Notable SCPs
- **SCP-173**: The original sculpture that moves when unobserved
- **SCP-682**: Hard-to-destroy reptile
- **SCP-999**: Friendly orange blob
- **SCP-087**: Endless stairwell
- **SCP-096**: Shy Guy that hunts those who see its face

## Historical Context

### Evolution
1. **2007**: SCP-173 posted on 4chan
2. **2008**: EditThis wiki created for organization
3. **2008**: Migration to Wikidot platform
4. **2010s**: Explosive growth in content and community
5. **2015+**: Mainstream recognition, games, and adaptations

### Creative Commons
The project uses Creative Commons licensing, allowing anyone to create derivative works while maintaining attribution requirements. This has facilitated the spread and adaptation of SCP content.

## Fiction vs. Folklore

### Documented Origins
Unlike traditional folklore with unclear origins, the SCP Foundation has:
- **Known Creation Date**: 2007 on 4chan
- **Identifiable Format**: Wiki-based collaborative fiction
- **Stated Fictional Nature**: Community acknowledges creative writing project
- **Transparent Process**: Public contribution and editing

### Folklore Characteristics
Despite documented origins, it exhibits folklore traits:
- **Oral/Digital Transmission**: Stories spread person-to-person online
- **Variation**: Multiple interpretations and retellings
- **Cultural Function**: Serves role of modern campfire stories
- **Community Ownership**: No single author, collective creation

## Modern Community
The SCP Foundation wiki hosts thousands of articles across multiple languages. Active community includes:
- Writers creating new SCPs
- Artists producing visual interpretations
- Game developers building experiences
- Moderators maintaining quality standards
- Readers engaging with the content
